# 4. Value Propositions
- Mission: Creating innovative solutions that empower restaurant operators to spend more time on the floor and less time in the office.
- a unique KPI dashboard and learning center that uses 60 days of data to calculate benchmark targets that empower operators to make informed, proactive decisions.
- Four tabs: SHIFT (comprehensive overview of live data for the day), VARIANCE (comparison between theoretical or baseline expectations and actual performance), PLAN (detailed forecast for each day of the week), BENCHMARK (Every 60 days, your targets will shift).
- LMS for training materials, handbooks, and assessments; chat bots that build a connected web of relationships; communication board (Daily Board, Announcements, Focus, Recognition, Coaching).
- Proactive metrics: CPLH, SPLH, PPA, Covers, Blended Wage, Table Turn Time; daypart breakdown (lunch, dinner, and late night); customized branding (operator logos, colors, app name).
# 5. Customer Relationships
- consultative, in-person approach with one-on-one, in-person interactions that foster genuine connections with clients.
- by utilizing our own technology within our restaurant, we can directly demonstrate to owners how our app enhances both profitability and operational efficiency.
- Onboarding bundles Integrations, Customization, Training, and Implementation.
- Tier 1 support via HelpScout customer support ticketing backed by Time etc vetted part-time virtual assistants.
# 6. Channels
- Marketing website (Webflow or Framer / SEO tooling); App Store Optimization via App Radar; product demo and training videos via Fiverr Pro.
- Sales and marketing platform: HubSpot CRM Suite to track every lead, Mailchimp for email nurture, and Google plus LinkedIn plus Meta ads.
- Sales materials via Vistaprint; Trade show travel and Industry conference attendance.
- Distribution: Apple Developer Program and Google Play Developer.
- Network: active participation in conferences across Canada and the United States.
# 7. Customer Segments
- a diverse range of restaurants eager to select their preferred technology solutions.
- high-level operators and clients of Benchmark Sixty - those who take a modern and proactive approach to data analytics within their establishments.
- restaurants aiming to enhance their staff development.
- Niche framing: Restaurants represent a unique niche market for technology solutions due to their distinct operational challenges, such as high labor and food costs, thin profit margins, and the necessity for rapid, multi-channel service.
