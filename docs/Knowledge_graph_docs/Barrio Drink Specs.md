# Drink Specs

## Espresso De Dulce De Leche

- **Tools Needed:** Small and Large Boston Shaker, Hawthorn Strainer, Fine Strainer, Jigger
- **Garnish/Rim:** Salted Caramel, 3 Espresso Beans

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/drink_specs/01.webp)
- **Glassware:** Gold Rim Coupe
- **Mixing Method:** Shake

Ingredients:

- 1.5oz Iceberg Vodka
- 0.5oz JD Shore Rum Cream
- 0.5oz Dulce De Leche Syrup
- 2oz Chilled Espresso

Directions:

1. Add ice and water to coupe and set aside.
2. Add ingredients to shaker with ice and shake vigorously.
3. Dump out ice/water mixture from coupe then double strain shaker contents into glass.
4. Put skewered caramel into drink. Top with espresso beans.

## Picoso Ananas

- **Tools Needed:** Small and Large Boston Shaker, Jigger, Hawthorn Strainer
- **Garnish/Rim:** Lime Wedge, Dehydrated Pineapple, Pineapple Frond, Black Salt Rim

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/drink_specs/02.webp)
- **Glassware:** Gold Deco Rocks Glass
- **Mixing Method:** Shake

Ingredients:

- 1oz Alida Blanco Tequila
- 0.5oz Triple Sec
- 0.5oz Jalapeno Syrup
- 0.5oz Pineapple Juice
- 0.75oz Lime Juice

Directions:

1. Put large square ice cube in rimmed glass.
2. Combine ingredients in shaker and shake vigorously.
3. Strain into glass.
4. Put lime wedge on side of glass with pineapple and frond.

## Alegría De Hierbas

- **Tools Needed:** Small and Large Boston Shaker, Hawthorn Strainer, Fine Strainer, Peeler, Jigger
- **Garnish/Rim:** Lemon Twist, Mint Sprig

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/drink_specs/03.webp)
- **Glassware:** Nick & Nora
- **Mixing Method:** Shake

Ingredients:

- 1.5oz Open Coast Gin
- 0.25oz Green Chartreuse
- 0.5oz Yerba Mate & Agave Syrup
- 1oz Lemon Juice

Directions:

1. Fill glass with ice and water then set aside.
2. Combine ingredients in shaker with ice and shake vigorously.
3. Dump out water and ice from glass then double strain shaker into glass.
4. Peel lemon, express on drink, twist and skewer with mint and place on glass.

## Cálidas Old Fashioned

- **Tools Needed:** Yarai Mixing Glass, Bar Spoon, Jigger, Hawthorn Strainer, Peeler
- **Garnish/Rim:** Orange Twist
- **Glassware:** Gold Deco Rocks Glass

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/drink_specs/04.webp)
- **Mixing Method:** Stir

Ingredients:

- 2oz Dos Maderas
- 0.5oz Agave Syrup
- 3 Drops Vanilla, Cardamom, Clove Extract

Directions:

1. Put large square ice cube in glass and set aside.
2. Add ingredients to yarai and stir for 30 seconds.
3. Strain yarai into glass.
4. Peel orange, express on drink, twist and place in drink.

## Sangria De Verano

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/drink_specs/05.webp)
- **Garnish/Rim:** Grapefruit Slice, Cherry
- **Glassware:** Wine Glass
- **Mixing Method:** Build

Ingredients:

- 3oz Red or White Wine
- 0.5oz Brandy
- 3oz Orange, Mango, Pineapple Juice
- 0.5oz Lime Juice
- Soda

Directions:

1. Fill glass with ice.
2. Add wine, brandy, juice, and syrup to glass then top with soda water and stir gently.
3. Place wheels down sides of glass, place cherry on ice, and grapefruit slice on glass rim.

## Coco Mojito

- **Tools Needed:** Small and Large Boston Shaker, Jigger, Hawthorn Strainer
- **Garnish/Rim:** Lime Wedge, Mint Sprig

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/drink_specs/09.webp)
- **Glassware:** Bamboo Glass
- **Mixing Method:** Shake

Ingredients:

- 1.5oz Iceberg White Rum
- 0.5oz Yerba Mate & Agave Syrup
- 2oz Coconut Milk
- 1oz Lime Juice
- 6 Mint Leaves
- 3oz Soda Water

Directions:

1. Lay a sprig of mint in the center of one palm and clap hands. Put in glass and fill with ice then set aside.
2. Put rum, syrup, lime, and cream of coconut in shaker with ice and shake vigorously. Strain into glass then top with soda.
3. Put lime wedge and mint sprig on rim of glass.

## Barrio Michelada

- **Tools Needed:** Jigger, Bar Spoon
- **Garnish/Rim:** Lime Wedge, Pickled Chili Pepper, Pickled Spicy Bean, Tajin Rim
- **Glassware:** Beer Glass
- **Mixing Method:** Build

Ingredients:

- 4oz Crown & Anchor
- 4oz Clamato Juice
- 3 Dashes Worcestershire Sauce
- 2 Dashes Valentina's
- 0.5oz Lime Juice
- Pinch Tajin

Directions:

1. Fill rimmed beer glass with ice. Add clamato, lime, valentina's and worcestershire to glass then top with beer and stir gently.
2. Add pinch of tajin to top of drink. Put spicy bean in drink.
3. Add lime wedge to side of glass and skewered pickled pepper.
