## Schedule and Availability

### My Schedule Screen

Opening the Push app lands you on the My Schedule screen. This shows the weekly Schedule you have been assigned.

#### Date Range

At the top on the grey bar you can choose the date range. Make sure you are looking at the correct date range for your schedule.

#### Shift Tag

Note the shift tag: This will show you the shift type. For example on call/until needed etc.

#### Comments

These are managerial notes for your shift. This can be station assignment e.g Fryer/Complete the cleaning list.

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/01.webp)

### Side Menu

Clicking the burger icon on the top left opens up a side menu.

#### Login Credentials

This shows your login credentials: The 4 digit number here is what you use to clock in and out on the tablet downstairs in the staff area.

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/02.webp)

### Daily Schedule

From the side bar:

Daily Schedule shows everyone that is scheduled for the day.

You can use the search bar at the top to see if someone is working that day and you can also adjust the day.

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/03.webp)

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/04.webp)

### Availability: Weekly Tab

Under Availability the first tab is Weekly: This allows you to set when you are available to work.

#### Available Check Box

Note the Available check box. Clicking on this means you are available in this time period set. Left Unchecked means you are unavailable in that time period.

#### Start and End Date

Start and end date can be set based on your discretion and agreement with your manager. If your availability varies weekly that can be set here weekly after discussing with your manager.

Alternatively you can set availability start and end date to any custom date range.

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/05.webp)

### Availability: Custom Tab

This is to set an availability within your normal availability that will expire and default back to the original availability that is set in weekly.

Reasons can be Exam week but going back to regular availability after.

Note Custom takes precedence over weekly on the scheduling side that the manager sees. So make sure you are communicating with management on this.

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/06.webp)

### Time Off

Time Off is used to send a request to management to not be scheduled for a particular date or date range.

This can be Vacation, exams, emergencies, illness etc.

Time offs must be approved by management before taken.

Note your availability defaults to what is set in weekly/custom once your time off period ends.

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/07.webp)

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/08.webp)

## Hours and Shift Management

### Hours Worked

Hours worked shows what hours you have clocked in for.

All clocked in hours need to go through Manager approval first.

The screen will show manager approved hours at the top and approval status on the shift display.

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/09.webp)

Once a manager approves a shift the Shift display turns green and the approved hours get displayed at the top.

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/10.webp)

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/11.webp)

### My Swaps

My Swaps Shows shifts that the you have requested to swap with a teammate.

The status of the swap will show in yellow.

Status 2; Pending Manager approval.

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/12.webp)

### Swap Requests

Swap Requests shows shifts that your teammates have requested to swap with you.

You can approve the request on your end here.

Make sure all shift swaps go through the Push app.

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/13.webp)

### Initializing a Swap Request

To initialize a swap request:

Navigate back to the My schedule screen and click on the arrow on the shift you want to swap.

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/14.webp)

This will open up a swaps screen.

You can switch shifts here with a teammate that is also assigned the same shift type.

For example a bartender can swap with another bartender. A bartender cannot swap with a server unless Manager authorized.

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/15.webp)

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/16.webp)

### Shift Releases

Shift releases allows you to give up your shift into a pool for a teammate to pickup.

Note you are fully responsible for your shift release all the way up to manager approval.

If not approved by a manager the shift defaults to its original scheduled state.

#### My Releases

My releases shows shift you have submitted into the pool.

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/17.webp)

### Release Pool

This shows all the shifts that have been put up for 'auction' by other teammates.

These are available for you to pickup.

Pickup also goes through manager approval.

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/18.webp)

### Releasing a Shift

To release a shift navigate back to my schedule, click on the arrow icon on the shift you want to release and click on releases on the next screen.

Add a note before sending it over for the manger to approve.

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/19.webp)

Once shifts are release or swap requests are made they will apear with a yellow status banner below. The 2 banners as mentioned above are:

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/20.webp)

1. pending user approval
2. pending manager approval

Note that on this screen you will also see shifts that other teammate's have requested you to swap with.

Click "Accept" to confirm the shift swap request.

Click on "Cancel" to reject the shift swap request.

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/21.webp)

Once any release or swap is accepted by a teammate it has to go through manager approval.

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/22.webp)

![](assets/internal/barrio/training/push_employee_sop/23.webp)

Always check with management.

### Bonus

You can sync your schedule with your ios calendar app or google calender

On an iPhone:

- Settings > Calendar > Accounts > Add Account > Other
- Tap "Add Subscribed Calendar"
- Paste the URL, tap Next, then Save

On Google Calendar (must be done on a computer, not the phone app):

- Go to calendar.google.com
- Next to "Other calendars" click the + , then "From URL"
- Paste the URL and click "Add calendar"
