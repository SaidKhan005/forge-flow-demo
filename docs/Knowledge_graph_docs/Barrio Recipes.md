## Aji Amarillo Dressing Yield 1L -  Shelf Life 5 - 7 Days

- 500mL Olive Oil
- 1 Jar Aji Amarillo
- 50ml Water ( Add to jar and shake)
- 2 Stalks Celery
- 4 Cloves of garlic
- ¼ Red Onion
- 10g Ginger
- 4 Juiced Limes
- Salt TT

### METHOD

In a vitamix, on the highest speed setting. Add all ingredients except for Olive Oil. Blend until smooth.

Drop speed to 5-6. Continuously and confidently pour the oil until emulsified. The entirety of Olive oil should take 30-45 seconds max.

Add to squeeze bottle. Label, Date

Chef Tips : WATCH THE VORTEX. As long as you see a deep whirlpool/vortex in the center, the emulsion is holding and drawing the oil down.

If the vortex starts to disappear and the dressing starts to spin in place or shiny on top. STOP POURING IMMEDIATELY.  The machine has gotten too hot or has received too much oil at once. Turn it off, throw an ice cube or two to cool down the dressing and re-emulsify.

## Burnt Onion Oil Yield 500mL – Shelf Life 2 Weeks

## Court Bouillon
