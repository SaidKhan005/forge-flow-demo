### Slide 12

- MAINS
- Lomo Saltado
- Lomo Saltado is one of Peru's most iconic dishes and a defining example of Chifa cuisine the fusion of Chinese and Peruvian culinary traditions.
- What is Chifa?
- Chifa refers to the unique cuisine that developed when Cantonese immigrants introduced Chinese cooking techniques and ingredients to Peru, blending them with local Peruvian flavors.
- Background
- Lomo Saltado originated in Lima, Peru, during the late 19th and early 20th centuries. Cantonese immigrants from Guangdong (southern China) brought the wok, high-heat stir-frying, and ingredients such as soy sauce. These techniques were combined with Peruvian beef, onions, tomatoes, ají peppers, and native potatoes, creating one of Peru's most celebrated fusion dishes. Today, it is widely recognized as a symbol of Peru's multicultural culinary heritage.

- Tagliatelle noodle: Italian ribbon pasta; long and flat: made from sheets of fresh egg dough;
- Tagliatelle translates to one precise ribbon
- Fun fact: It comes from Emilia-Romagna, the region around Bologna, Italy where it's taken so seriously that a golden ruler defining the official ribbon width is kept at the city's Chamber of Commerce.

- Background: Tagliatelle crossed over to Latin America in 1870-1930. In Spanish the name loosened to tallarines which is a general word for any long noodle, spaghetti, wok noodles, ribbon pasta.
- Poblano Crema: Poblano Creamy Green Chile Sauce -> Burn skin off mild green pepper over a flame -> Comes from same Mixtera region as Masa
- Crema -> Mexican table cream. Lighter than sour cream

- Brasa Means Ember in Spanish:
- Polo a la Brasa: Peru's Charcoal Rotisserie Chicken
- Fun Fact: A swiss settler named Roger Scheler started roasting Chicken this way outside Lima Peru. A Swiss Engineer friend built him a rotating system. - Chimichurri: Argentina's signature herb sauce, traditionally served with Asado (barbecue). Its flavor deepens as the ingredients meld over time.
- Asado: The traditional open-fire grilling culture of Argentina's Pampas region.
