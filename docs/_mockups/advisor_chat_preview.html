Why was my labor cost over target on Saturday?
Here is what I would consider

Saturday ran about 4 labor hours over your target for the day. Two things stand out:

  • The dinner daypart had three people on the floor when your plan called for two. That is most of the gap.
  • Sales came in close to plan, so this looks like a scheduling overlap, not a slow night.

You could look at the dinner shift overlap next Saturday, or check whether the extra hand was a planned cover. I am pointing at what the numbers show; the call is yours.
