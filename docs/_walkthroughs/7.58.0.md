## Demo-mode walkthrough

### Step 1 — Launch the operator app in demo mode

`lib/main_forgeflow.dart` boots the operator shell with
`MockReplayDataSeed` attached. Default business date is `2026-03-27`
(Friday in W13).

### Step 2 — Variance > This Week (closed-week regression check)

Tap **Variance**. The PRIMARY DRIVER section reads
`weekData.primaryLeverId`, computed by `LaborModel.determineLever` on
WTD-aggregated closed shifts (Mon–Thu).
`variance_this_week_tab.dart:90` does
`LeverCards.all.firstWhere((l) => l.id == weekData.primaryLeverId, …)`
and renders the `LeverCardWidget` for that id.

**Expected:** rendered `LeverCardData.id == weekData.primaryLeverId`
(pinned by the contract test's "closed row driver label matches
engine" group across 12 scenarios — `covers_*`, `ppa_*`, `cplh_*`,
`splh_*`, `foh_wage_*`, `boh_wage_*`).

### Step 3 — Variance > This Week > FULL WEEK PROJECTION (purity check)

Scroll to FULL WEEK PROJECTION. Tap **Mon** → expand: closed lunch
and dinner detail panels each render `_LeverBadge` (line 1194)
showing the row's own engine-derived lever. Closed-row truth
unchanged.

Tap **Fri** → expand. Fri lunch is closed → renders its own
`_LeverBadge`. Fri dinner / late_night are projected → no
`_LeverBadge`; `ProjectionDaypartRow.driverLabel` is
`'Not yet available'`.

**Expected (regression check):** Fri dinner / late_night
`driverLabel == 'Not yet available'` even though the seeded
`primaryLever` is `'ON_MODEL'`. No silent fall-through to
`covers_down`. Pinned by the new test's "open / projected row
purity" group, including a defensive scenario where storage carries
a real lever id (simulating the pre-`7.58.5` carry-forward bug).
