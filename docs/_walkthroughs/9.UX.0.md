# 9.UX.0 Walkthrough Trace

Date: 2026-04-29
Mode: kDemoMode = true
Evidence type: text trace

1. Launch ForgeFlow.
2. AuthGate lands on the branded Forge & Flow login screen with the splash icon, sunset action styling, email and password fields, forgot-password action, and the demo operator shortcut.
3. Select "Use demo operator".
4. The login form fills the demo operator credentials and submits through AuthSessionNotifier.signInWithEmailPassword.
5. If MFA is required, AuthGate switches to the MFA challenge screen. Enter the six-digit authenticator code and submit with "Verify".
6. A successful auth result creates an AuthSession and AuthGate renders the ForgeFlow app shell.
7. Open Settings from the app shell.
8. Settings shows the Account tab for the signed-in operator. The Account tab exposes Change Password, Sign Out, Sign Out Everywhere, and the Phase 9.8 Terms & Conditions placeholder.
9. PermissionContext is loaded through AuthPermissionContextBridge. ForgeFlow maps allowed permission effects into a TeamScopeActor.
10. With team.users.view allowed for an owner or scoped manager, Settings shows the Team tab.
11. Open Team. The section loads users, role options, and pending invites through AuthOperationsGateway.
12. Enter a new user email, choose a role, choose operator or location scope, and send the invite.
13. Open the row action menu for an active user and choose Suspend. Confirm the dialog; the action calls suspendUser through the gateway.
14. Open the row action menu for the suspended user and choose Reactivate. Confirm the dialog; the action calls reactivateUser through the gateway.
15. Open the row action menu and choose Change role. Save the dialog; the shell revokes the previous grant when present and creates the replacement grant.
16. Open the row action menu and choose Revoke role. Confirm the dialog; the action calls revokeRoleGrant.
17. Repeat Settings with an authenticated operator whose permission snapshot does not allow team.users.view.
18. The Account tab remains visible for the signed-in operator, while the Team tab is hidden. Direct Team actions remain disabled unless the matching Team permission and scope checks allow them.

Permission-gating result: a user without Team view permission cannot open the Team tab, and action buttons are locked by the specific invite, suspend, reactivate, reset-password, assign-role, and revoke-role permissions.
