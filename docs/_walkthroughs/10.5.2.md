This walkthrough lights up what 10.5.0's surface scaffold and 10.5.1's
bucketing engine were waiting on:

- `ShiftServicePeriodReadService` — pure-function accumulator that
  consumes 10.5.1's `DaypartBucketer` and folds canonical POS lines
  + labor punches into per-service-period buckets (covers, sales,
  FOH/BOH minutes, wage dollars, derived CPLH / SPLH / PPA / blended
  wage). The accumulator honors the Jim Taylor labor-punch-split
  rule by dropping `non_service` slivers from the per-period
  denominators.
- `ShiftServicePeriodNotifier` — runtime state holder. Synthesizes
  canonical facts from the existing `OpenShiftSnapshot` records in
  demo (one POS line at the period midpoint, one FOH + one BOH
  punch sized to the snapshot's scheduled hours). When Phase 8 ships,
  the synthesizer is replaced by a real fact loader; the notifier
  API and read service stay the same.
- Shift dashboard SERVICE PERIODS cards now render the per-period
  metrics (`COVERS · SALES`, `PPA · CPLH · SPLH`, `HRS · BLENDED
  WAGE`) in place of 10.5.0's "Daypart bucketing not yet wired."
  placeholder. Empty buckets (zero-data periods) show "No data yet
  for this period." instead of misleading zeros. Missing restaurant
  timezone renders explicit unavailable copy instead of the generic
  no-data line.
- Time-into-service header strip ("Lunch · 1h 12m in") sits above
  the SERVICE PERIODS sticky group. Renders only when a service
  period is in progress; hidden in gaps and when the restaurant has
  no usable IANA timezone.
- Variance "This Week" tab grows a `Whole Week | Daypart` scope
  toggle. Default = Whole Week (existing WTD vs Plan + Dollar Impact
  + Primary Driver + Full Week Projection are byte-identical).
  Daypart opens a per-service-period lens reading from the same
  `ShiftServicePeriodNotifier` — Shift and Variance are now
  reconciliable by construction (single accumulator source).

## Engine boundary semantics — recap from 10.5.1

The accumulator inherits the bucketer's semantics; nothing new here:

1. **Point-in-time facts (POS lines)** are bucketed inclusively at
   both period endpoints with sub-minute precision. An order at
   exactly `15:00:00` belongs to Lunch (per the phase doc's locked
   example). `15:00:00.001` and beyond fall into the post-Lunch gap
   and are dropped from every bucket.
2. **Labor punches** are split into per-period segments using the
   half-open convention at the upper end. The phase doc's worked
   example reconciles exactly: a `14:30 → 19:00` punch yields
   `lunch[14:30, 15:00) = 30 min` + `non_service[15:00, 17:00) =
   120 min` + `dinner[17:00, 19:00) = 120 min`. The 120 non-service
   minutes are emitted by the bucketer as a `null`-period segment
   and are **dropped** by the accumulator (Jim Taylor rule).
3. **Late Night cross-midnight** punches stay attributed to the
   originating business date. A `Sat 23:30 → Sun 01:30` punch lands
   wholly in `late_night` on Saturday's business day.
4. **Missing IANA timezone** propagates as `MissingTimezoneError`
   when the engine is invoked. The notifier catches it, sets
   `missingTimezone = true`, and renders an empty bucket map so the
   UI can display an honest "timezone not configured" message
   instead of silently zeroed metrics.

## Demo synthesis — Phase 8 stand-in

Until Phase 8's vendor connectors land, the notifier synthesizes
canonical facts from the existing `OpenShiftSnapshot` records:

- For each closed/open snapshot for the current business date:
  - Look up the matching service-period definition (`s.daypart` →
    `definition`).
  - Compute the period's calendar `[start, end)` interval (mirroring
    `DaypartBucketer`'s internal `_periodIntervalsOnBusinessDate`
    logic).
  - Emit one POS line at the midpoint with covers + sales =
    `currentCovers × currentPPA`.
  - Emit one FOH labor punch sized to `scheduledFohHours × 60`
    minutes starting at `start` (capped at the period length so the
    bucketer never sees a punch reaching outside its owning period).
  - Emit one BOH labor punch the same way.
  - Wage rate = `blendedWage` for both roles (the snapshot only
    carries the blended rate; FOH/BOH separation arrives with
    Phase 8 vendor data).

This synthesizer is exposed as `synthesizeCanonicalFactsFromSnapshots`
under `@visibleForTesting`. When Phase 8 ships, the notifier swaps
this call for a real fact loader; the read service and accumulator
do not change.

### Edge — Restaurant has no usable IANA timezone

If `RestaurantLocation.businessTimezone` is empty or unrecognized:

- `ShiftServicePeriodNotifier.missingTimezone` becomes `true` and
  `buckets` is populated with empty accumulators (one per defined
  period).
- The Shift daypart section renders an explicit notice:
  `"Restaurant timezone is not configured. Per-period metrics are
  unavailable until Settings is completed."`
- Each Shift daypart card renders `"Timezone not configured — metrics
  unavailable."` instead of the generic `No data yet for this period.`
  placeholder.
- The time-into-service header is suppressed (mirrors
  `current_state_boundary_monitor.dart`'s posture: refuse to fall
  back to the device clock).
- The Variance daypart lens shows an explicit notice:
  `"Restaurant timezone is not configured. Per-period numbers are
  unavailable until Settings has been completed."`

### Edge — Correction replay (Phase 8 hook, demo path inert)

The notifier exposes `applyPosLineCorrection` and
`applyLaborPunchCorrection` so that, once Phase 8 vendor
connectors ship, individual fact corrections (Toast
`Order.modifiedDate`, 7shifts `time_punch.edited` /
`time_punch.deleted`) can update the affected period's bucket
without rebuilding the full fact list. The demo synthesizer
rebuilds in full on `refresh()` and does not exercise these methods
yet — they are unit-tested directly in
`test/services/shift_service_period_read_service_test.dart`.

## Boundary checks

- **Whole-day Shift surface is byte-identical when scope =
  Whole Day.** `_wholeDaySlivers(rm)` is unchanged; the existing
  `shift_visual_widget_test.dart` smoke suite still passes verbatim.
- **Whole-day `ShiftDashboardNotifier` and `ShiftDashboardReadModel`
  are not touched.** The new notifier (`ShiftServicePeriodNotifier`)
  is additive and reads from the same SQLite tables.
- **No new schema.** Canonical facts are synthesized from the
  existing `open_shift_snapshots` table; no migrations were added.
  The migration drift scanner does not need to run for this slice.
- **No primary-driver teaching changes.** 10.5.3+ owns the daypart-
  live primary driver. The Variance daypart lens shows per-period
  actuals only; the existing whole-week PRIMARY DRIVER section is
  unchanged on the Whole Week branch.
- **Settings → Timing editor is not added.** Persisted timing config
  remains a deferred-foundation prerequisite per the 10.5 plan; the
  notifier falls back to `demoDefinitions` and the
  `_defaultBusinessDayStartLocalTime` (`04:00`) constant when no
  timing config is persisted.
- **Refresh policy.** `AppRefreshCoordinator.refreshCurrentStateSurfaces`
  fires `_shiftServicePeriod?.refresh()` alongside the existing
  `_weekData.refresh()` and `_shiftDashboard.refresh()` calls so the
  per-period buckets stay in sync with active-target changes,
  invalidation-bus writes, and Settings reseeds. Shift pull-to-refresh
  also awaits both whole-day and per-period notifiers.
- **Review fixes folded in.** Active-period resolution uses full
  sub-minute precision; Shift missing-timezone copy is explicit;
  manual Shift refresh updates service-period buckets; POS check
  corrections use explicit `checkDelta`.
- **No `kDemoMode` branching.** The notifier and read service are
  demo-mode-agnostic. The demo synthesizer is the only difference
  vs the future Phase 8 path; it lives behind a private helper and
  swaps cleanly when the real fact tables exist.

## Notes for 10.5.3+ (daypart-live primary driver)

- The accumulator already exposes everything 10.5.3 needs: per-period
  covers, sales, FOH/BOH minutes, wage dollars, plus derived
  CPLH/SPLH/PPA/blended-wage. The lever determination
  (`LaborModel.determineLever`) can be invoked per-period directly.
- Per-period plan targets (forecast covers, FOH/BOH model hours)
  are not yet shipped — `ActiveTargetProfile` carries whole-day
  targets only. 10.5.3 will need either a per-period extension to
  the active target profile or a synthesis from the SchedulePlan
  day row's daypart breakdown.
- The `DaypartEvidenceVisibilityPolicy` from 7.55k.7 should gate
  the per-period primary-driver display when the in-period sample
  is too thin.
- The `_VarianceScopeToggle` and the `SERVICE PERIODS · TODAY`
  group on the Variance tab are the natural surface for daypart-
  live primary driver — no new shell changes required.
