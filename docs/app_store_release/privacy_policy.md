# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: April 1, 2026**

This privacy policy applies to the Forge & Flow and Barrio mobile applications ("Apps") developed by Forge & Flow ("we", "us", or "our").

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information.**

The Apps operate entirely on your device. All data — including shift records, baselines, targets, and settings — is stored locally in an on-device database and is never sent to any server.

## Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using SQLite. This includes:
- Shift records and history
- Baseline and target configurations
- User preferences and settings

No data is transmitted over the internet. The Apps do not require an internet connection to function.

## Third-Party Services

The Apps do not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not use:
- Analytics SDKs
- Advertising networks
- Crash reporting services that transmit data externally
- Social media integrations

## Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data.

## Children's Privacy

The Apps are business tools intended for restaurant managers and are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

## Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by:
- Clearing the app's data through your device settings, or
- Uninstalling the app

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Apps after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
