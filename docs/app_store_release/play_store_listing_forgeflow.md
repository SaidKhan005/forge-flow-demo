## Full Description (max 4000 chars)

Forge & Flow is the labor-management coaching app built on the Jim Taylor labor model. It turns real shift data into clear, actionable decisions — so restaurant managers know exactly which lever to pull, every shift.

**What it does:**

This Week
See your week-to-date labor picture at a glance. Forge & Flow diagnoses which lever matters most right now — covers, PPA, CPLH, SPLH, scheduling, or wages — and tells you where to focus before your next shift.

History
Spot the patterns that repeat week after week. Color-coded daypart analysis shows which leaks keep coming back, so you stop fixing symptoms and start fixing systems.

Learn
Benchmarks, recurring patterns, and repeatable wins — summarized into teaching moments you can share with your team.

Baseline & Targets
Set your restaurant's baseline from real historical performance. Targets lock at shift close so your variance analysis always compares against what was true at the time.

Schedule Builder
Plan labor hours against your targets before the week starts. See projected CPLH and SPLH before a single shift is worked.

Shift Dashboard
Live shift view with model hours, actual hours, dollar gap, and OPZ status. Know where you stand while you can still act.

**Built on the Jim Taylor Model:**
- Covers per Labor Hour (CPLH)
- Sales per Labor Hour (SPLH)
- Per Person Average (PPA)
- Theoretical Labor Percentage
- Optimal Productivity Zone (OPZ)
- Model Hours vs. Actual Hours variance

**Who it's for:**
Restaurant general managers, assistant managers, and multi-unit operators who want to coach labor — not just track it.

**No login required. No subscription. Your data stays on your device.**
