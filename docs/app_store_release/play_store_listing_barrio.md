## Full Description (max 4000 chars)

Barrio is the internal operations app for Barrio Legado — a single hub where managers access training, playbooks, and labor coaching tools built for the way your restaurant actually runs.

**What's inside:**

Jim Taylor Labor Model
The full Forge & Flow labor coaching system, embedded directly. Diagnose your week, read shift variances, set baselines, and coach your team on covers, PPA, CPLH, and SPLH — all grounded in real shift data.

Company Handbook
Your operating standards, policies, and procedures — organized by chapter and always available offline. No more digging through shared drives or binders.

Interview Playbook
Structured interview guides and evaluation frameworks so every hiring conversation is consistent and thorough.

El Podio (Coming Soon)
Recognition and team performance tracking.

Preston Lee Model (Coming Soon)
Advanced operational modeling for multi-unit leaders.

**Built for Barrio Legado managers:**
- Beautiful glassmorphism UI designed for quick daily use
- Animated ambient backgrounds that make the app feel alive
- Bubble navigation for fast access to every learning surface
- Works offline — your data stays on your device

**Labor Coaching powered by the Jim Taylor Model:**
- This Week — diagnose which lever matters most right now
- History — spot patterns that repeat week over week
- Learn — benchmarks and repeatable wins
- Baseline & Targets — set from real performance
- Schedule Builder — plan labor hours before the week starts
- Shift Dashboard — model vs. actual in real time

**No login required. No subscription.**
