Architecture pass

  • Dayparts not hardcoded: the lens, summary scope, calendar count, breakdown rows and band all derive from TIMING_CONFIG.servicePeriodDefinitions. Change the config and the whole screen follows. Mirrors ServicePeriodDefinitionResolver reading restaurant_timing_configs.
  • Whole day wage only: no per period wage. Per period labor dollars = period hours times whole day wage (Design Rule 5).
  • No parallel stack: Lean/Balanced/Generous just writes selected recordKeys through the existing manager override path. No strictness column, no second pool formula.
  • Whole day = rollup: cover weighted from the per period selected shifts. Covers/hours/sales sum, CPLH/SPLH/PPA cover weighted, verified reconciling.
  • Override gate surfaced: "one override per 60 day cycle" shown before Done, not as an after the fact error.
